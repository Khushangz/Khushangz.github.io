At Athaca, I build applied AI systems for pharmaceutical and clinical-trial workflows, with a focus on turning complex protocol and trial data into structured, verifiable information that can support patient finding and eligibility assessment.

My work includes developing agentic pipelines that extract and validate eligibility criteria from AACT and clinical trial protocols, combining LLM-based extraction with verification mechanisms to improve reliability. I also contribute to an AI-powered clinical trial platform supporting protocol intelligence, trial benchmarking, and patient eligibility estimation.

On the AI infrastructure side, I shipped an MCP server integrating Claude Code into internal engineering workflows and developed RAG-based capabilities incorporated into the core product. I am also researching world models and synthetic patient twins for pharmaceutical applications and simulation-driven analysis.
